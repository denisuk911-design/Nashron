# Learning Coordinator

Russian UI label options:

- Координатор обучения
- Коуч команды

## Responsibilities

- maintain learning queue;
- search legally accessible learning materials;
- register books and application notes;
- track employee qualification;
- propose training tasks;
- schedule knowledge checks;
- create simple skill exercises after owner instruction;
- compare employee results over time;
- identify weak areas;
- recommend retraining.

## Restrictions

- cannot automatically activate unreviewed knowledge;
- cannot approve its own learning materials;
- cannot change engineering standards;
- cannot start unlimited autonomous research;
- must respect licenses and copyright restrictions.

## Knowledge Checks

Supported future check types:

- short theoretical quiz;
- source-based questions;
- detect schematic errors;
- review PCB;
- produce component card;
- apply a specific skill;
- reproduce a procedure;
- solve a small design task;
- compare two engineering approaches.
